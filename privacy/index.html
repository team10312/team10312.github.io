Privacy Policy

This page explains what Annalytix collects through this website, how that information is used, and how to contact us with privacy questions.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly through forms on this website, including names, email addresses, and message details when you contact the team or submit interest forms.

We may also collect basic technical information automatically through normal website operation, such as browser type, device information, referring pages, and approximate usage analytics.

How We Use Information

  • Respond to messages, sponsorship inquiries, and team interest submissions.
  • Operate and improve the website, including form handling and page performance.
  • Coordinate team communications, outreach, and event-related logistics when applicable.

Sharing And Storage

We do not sell personal information. Information submitted through the site may be processed by service providers we use to operate forms, hosting, analytics, and scouting tools.

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for team operations, communication history, legal compliance, or legitimate administrative purposes.

Children And Student Data

Annalytix is a school-affiliated robotics team. We aim to handle student-related information responsibly and limit collection to what is necessary for team participation, communication, and public team operations.

Your Choices

If you want to update or delete information you previously shared through this website, contact the team using the link below so we can review the request.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how information is handled, visit /#join and send us a message.
